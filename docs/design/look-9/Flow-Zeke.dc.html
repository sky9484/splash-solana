How Zeke is kept safe

  • It can only read and draft. It can't approve, pay or change a rule.
  • Every draft is checked against your rules again before anyone approves it.
  • Emergency stop is ready: one switch stops all drafting.
  • It can be wrong. Every field shows where it came from, so you can check.
