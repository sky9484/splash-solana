US Treasury bills

USDY, issued by Ondo
3.75%a year variable · set monthly by Ondo · as of 13 Oct 2026 · source

A note backed by short-term US Treasury bills and bank deposits. Not a bank deposit; its value can fall.

  • You subscribe with US dollars. Your dollars go to Ondo, and Ondo issues USDY units into your Investments account. Splash only builds the order; you approve it.
  • You keep the same number of units. Each unit's price rises daily as interest builds up.
  • Subscribed straight from Ondo: units are held 40 days before you can redeem them. On the market: no hold, for smaller amounts.
  • For non-US companies only.

Risk and rules

  • These are your company's own investments, in your own account. Splash doesn't hold, manage or advise on them.
  • Not available to US persons. Before your first subscription or buy, an owner confirms no owner, director or approver is one.
  • Your company rules: reserve floor 10% above what's due · stocks at most 20% of treasury. Set by your owner; changing them comes with the rules update.
  • Sweep, when it's on: Zeke drafts each move from your owner's rule (keep the reserve at its floor and at least US$5,000.00 in Payments). Every move needs an approval. Zeke never picks an investment.
  • Splash fee on subscribing, redeeming, buying and selling: US$0.
